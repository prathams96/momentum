# Product

## Register

product

## Platform

ios

## Users

Momentum is for one Routine Owner: a 30-year-old student using an iPhone 16 Pro Max to run a highly personalized fitness, nutrition, skincare, and hair-care system. The app is opened in short bursts at the gym, around meals, and during morning and evening care. There are no accounts, coaches, communities, or secondary users.

## Product Purpose

Momentum turns today's obligations into a small, concrete sequence: complete the next recovery-appropriate workout, log food, perform due care routines, and see consistency accumulate. Success means the Routine Owner can understand what is due in seconds, record it without ceremony, and trust the app to preserve the correct next action when life disrupts the plan.

## Positioning

Momentum is a private personal execution system that follows the body's recovery cycle instead of forcing routines into a Monday-reset calendar.

## Brand Personality

Direct, disciplined, and quietly encouraging. Copy is brief and candid, never clinical, childish, macho, or guilt-inducing. Celebration is earned and restrained: the product acknowledges consistency without turning self-care into a casino.

## Anti-references

- Gym-bro interfaces built from black, neon green, flames, aggressive slogans, or pseudo-military language.
- Childish gamification with coins, cartoon mascots, confetti on routine actions, streak freezes, or manipulative loss warnings.
- Sterile medical dashboards that make ordinary self-care feel diagnostic.
- Web-dashboard card grids transplanted onto an iPhone, especially nested cards, tiny labels, and custom navigation.
- Decorative glassmorphism, gradients, and motion that compete with logging a task.

## Design Principles

1. **Today before totals.** The next useful action is more important than an impressive dashboard.
2. **Sequence before weekdays.** Fitness scheduling reflects completed work and recovery, never a Monday reset.
3. **Fast input, durable history.** Common actions take a few taps; past records retain the context in which they were created.
4. **Accountability without shame.** Misses are recorded honestly, recovery is neutral, and copy always points to the next action.
5. **Native restraint.** Standard iOS structure and controls carry the interaction; brand appears through hierarchy, tint, data visualization, and carefully chosen moments.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Momentum targets WCAG 2.2 AA-equivalent contrast and full native iOS accessibility behavior. It supports Dynamic Type through accessibility sizes, VoiceOver, Reduce Motion, Reduce Transparency, Increased Contrast, 44-by-44-point minimum touch targets, light and dark appearances, and non-color status cues. Health and adherence language remains descriptive rather than diagnostic or moralizing.

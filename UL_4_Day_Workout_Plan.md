# 4-Day Upper/Lower Workout Plan

## Weekly Schedule

| Day | Workout |
|---|---|
| Monday | Upper A |
| Tuesday | Lower A |
| Wednesday | Rest |
| Thursday | Upper B |
| Friday | Rest |
| Saturday | Lower B |
| Sunday | Rest |

> If a workout is missed, continue with the next workout in sequence instead of resetting every Monday.

## Upper A

**Focus:** Incline pressing, horizontal pulling, shoulders, and arms

| Exercise | Sets | Rep Range | Notes |
|---|---:|---:|---|
| Incline Dumbbell Press | 3 | 6–10 | Main chest press |
| Cable Row | 3 | 8–12 | Keep torso stable |
| Assisted Pull-Up | 2 | 6–10 | Controlled full range |
| Dumbbell Shoulder Press | 2 | 6–10 | Avoid excessive back arch |
| Cable Lateral Raise | 2 | 12–20 | Controlled movement |
| Cable Overhead Triceps Extension | 2 | 10–15 | Long-head triceps focus |
| Face-Away Cable Curl | 2 | 10–15 | Keep upper arm fixed |

## Lower A

**Focus:** Quadriceps, hamstrings, calves, and core

| Exercise | Sets | Rep Range | Notes |
|---|---:|---:|---|
| Leg Press | 3 | 8–12 | Main lower-body movement |
| Dumbbell Goblet Squat | 2–3 | 8–12 | Keep torso controlled |
| Leg Extension | 2 | 10–15 | Pause near full extension |
| Seated or Lying Leg Curl | 3 | 8–15 | Controlled eccentric |
| Machine or Leg-Press Calf Raise | 3 | 10–20 | Full stretch and contraction |
| Pallof Press or Dead Bug | 2–3 | 8–15 | Core stability |

## Progression Rules

1. Use a weight that allows the lower end of the rep range with good form.
2. Stop most compound sets with approximately **1–2 reps in reserve**.
3. Isolation exercises may be taken closer to failure.
4. When all sets reach the top of the rep range, increase the weight slightly.
5. Do not attempt a true one-rep maximum during normal workout sessions.
6. Record weight, reps, completed sets, and perceived effort after each exercise.

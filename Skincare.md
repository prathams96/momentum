# Daily Skincare Routine

## 🌞 Morning (Daily)

### If face feels oily
- [ ] Wash face with Pilgrim Salicylic Acid Face Wash

### If face feels normal/dry
- [ ] Rinse face with water only

Then:

- [ ] Apply Pilgrim Ceramide Moisturizer (thin layer)
- [ ] Wait 30-60 seconds
- [ ] Apply Conscious Chemist SPF 50 Sunscreen

---

## 🌙 Night

### Monday & Thursday (Adapalene Nights)

- [ ] Wash face with Pilgrim Salicylic Acid Face Wash
- [ ] Pat dry
- [ ] Wait 10-20 minutes
- [ ] Apply Adapalene (pea-sized amount over entire face)
- [ ] Wait 2-5 minutes
- [ ] Apply Pilgrim Ceramide Moisturizer

---

### Wednesday & Saturday (Azelaic Acid Nights)

- [ ] Wash face with Pilgrim Salicylic Acid Face Wash
- [ ] Apply Underated Azelaic Acid Cream (pea-sized amount)
- [ ] Wait 1-2 minutes
- [ ] Apply Pilgrim Ceramide Moisturizer

---

### Tuesday, Friday & Sunday (Recovery Nights)

- [ ] Wash face with Pilgrim Salicylic Acid Face Wash
- [ ] Apply Pilgrim Ceramide Moisturizer only

---

# Hair Routine

## Wednesday & Saturday

- [ ] Plum Salicylic Acid Shampoo
- [ ] Massage scalp gently for 1-2 minutes
- [ ] Rinse thoroughly
- [ ] Apply Tresemmé Keratin Conditioner to hair lengths only (avoid scalp)
- [ ] Rinse after 2-3 minutes

---

# Gym Days

If sweating heavily:

- [ ] Wash face after workout
- [ ] Reapply sunscreen if going back outdoors

---

# After 2 Weeks

If adapalene is well tolerated:

Increase adapalene to:

- Monday
- Thursday
- Sunday

Replace the recovery night on Sunday.

---

# After 6-8 Weeks

If acne is still active:

Increase adapalene gradually to every other night.

Keep moisturizer and sunscreen daily.
